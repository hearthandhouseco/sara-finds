Check out this SmartWings find

SmartWings makes custom motorized blinds and shades designed to bring smart-home control to everyday window treatments. Depending on the motor and product you choose, you can control your shades with a remote, app, voice commands, schedules, scenes, and supported smart-home systems.

What caught my attention

  • Custom-made shades for different window sizes and spaces
  • Blackout, light-filtering, and sunscreen options
  • Motorized control with multiple smart-home compatibility options
  • Selected motor options support Matter integration
  • Remote, app, voice, scheduling, and scene-control options
  • Cordless designs are available
  • Free shipping with no minimum, according to SmartWings
  • 3-year warranty on motors and controls
